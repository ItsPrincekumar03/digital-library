# Digital Library — Vision and Scope

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the long-term vision, product direction, scope boundaries, target users, major capabilities, and development principles of the Digital Library project.

It provides a high-level view of what the system is intended to become while clearly separating the current V1 scope from future possibilities.

This document should be used together with the Product Requirements Document (PRD), Software Requirements Specification (SRS), technical documentation, and current source code.

---

# 2. Product Vision

The vision of the Digital Library project is to provide a centralized, secure, accessible, and user-friendly digital platform where users can discover and read digital books through a structured online library.

The system aims to combine traditional library-management capabilities with modern digital reading features.

The long-term product concept is:

```text
Digital Library
      ↓
Book Discovery
      ↓
Structured Content
      ↓
Online Reading
      ↓
Personalized Reading
      ↓
Audio / TTS
      ↓
Translation
      ↓
Intelligent Assistance
