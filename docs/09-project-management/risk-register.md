# Digital Library — Risk Register

## 1. Document Purpose

This document identifies, assesses, monitors, and manages risks associated with the Digital Library project.

The purpose of the risk register is to help the project team identify problems before they become major project failures.

Risks may relate to:

- Requirements.
- Scope.
- Architecture.
- Development.
- Database.
- Security.
- Testing.
- Team coordination.
- Git/version control.
- Dependencies.
- Deployment.
- Performance.
- Reader functionality.
- TTS/audio.
- Translation.
- Academic submission.
- Documentation.

This document is a management tool.

It does not claim that a listed risk has occurred.
